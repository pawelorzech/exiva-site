4. What the service is

Exiva is a companion for Tibia players: track characters, watch guilds, houses and highscores, keep notes and goals, analyse hunt sessions, use calculators and an encyclopaedia, and receive notifications about events you define yourself.

Exiva is not, and will never be:

  • a gameplay automation tool (bot, macro, or script controlling the game client);
  • a tool integrating with the game client, capturing packets, or modifying the game;
  • a tool that requires or accepts your Tibia login credentials.

4.1. Free tier

Free of charge, available without an account or registration. It includes, among other things: tracking three characters refreshed on your own device, basic calculators, browsing the encyclopaedia and public data, limited history, notifications generated locally when the App or its background job wakes up, and broadcast notifications (news, events, daily digest).

The Free tier does not include push notifications sent from our server — you learn about an event when the App refreshes the data on your device.

In the Free tier we create no account and store none of your data on our server — everything stays on your device.

4.2. Premium tier

Paid, available after purchasing a Subscription. It includes, among other things: push notifications sent from our server around the clock, whether or not the App is running, tracking up to 50 characters, advanced alert rules, cross-device sync, full history, hunt session analyser, goals, offline encyclopaedia, house and auction watching, and extended guild tooling.

The current, binding feature scope of both tiers is presented in the App on the subscription screen before purchase. Feature scope may change in accordance with sections 9 and 19.

The App contains no ads. We have no plans to introduce them in any tier.

4.3. Technical requirements

Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer, an internet connection, and free storage space. Purchasing a Subscription requires a Google Play account with an available payment method. Notifications require Google Play Services. An Exiva Account requires a Google account.
