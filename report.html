Report a problem or infringement

Use this channel for security reports, copyright or trademark concerns, incorrect attribution, abusive content, or a serious factual problem in Exiva.

Email pawel@orzech.me

Include

  • the affected screen, URL or data source;
  • a concise description and steps to reproduce;
  • screenshots only if they contain no passwords, tokens or payment details;
  • for rights complaints, the work or mark concerned and your basis for contacting us.

Never send a Tibia password, Google password, purchase token, full card number or private key. Exiva support does not need them.

Security reports should start the subject with [SECURITY]. We acknowledge actionable reports as soon as reasonably possible.
