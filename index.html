What Exiva does

Name a character once. From then on Exiva watches the world for you: when they come online, when they gain a level, when they die and to whom.

Character tracking

Name a character once. From then on Exiva watches the world for you: when they come online, when they gain a level, when they die and to whom.

Alerts when something happens

You find out from a notification, not from scrolling a website an hour later. Free refreshes on your device, Premium watches on our server around the clock. Quiet hours, cooldowns and per-object muting keep alerts signal.

Worlds, guilds, houses

Search any character, guild, world or house on any server. Population, PvP type, BattlEye, transfers, server save.

Highscores and events

Highscores across every public category and vocation. Boosted creature, boosted boss and Rashid's location, every day.

Calculators

Hunting, blessing, exercise weapon, magic level, stamina, loot split and more — every formula documented and unit-tested.

Codex and offline mode

A creature, item, spell and imbuement codex you can actually search. Cached data stays readable on the metro, and every screen shows exactly how old it is.

Free and Premium

The difference is not how many slots you get. It is when you find out. Free refreshes your characters on your own device — you know once the app wakes up. Premium watches them on our server around the clock — you know within a minute, even if you have not opened the app in two days.

No advertising anywhere. No artificially slowed free experience. The paywall appears when you reach for a paid feature and nowhere else.

Built to be honest about data

Every record carries its source and its age. When something is stale, Exiva says so instead of pretending. When a source is down, you keep the last good data with a clear warning — you do not get an empty screen.

Privacy

No account is required to use Exiva. Free users have no server-side profile at all — your character, notes and settings stay on your device. Exiva never asks for your Tibia login and never stores it. Your watchlists are private.
