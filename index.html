Straight answers

Before you ask.

Still unsure? Write to pawel@orzech.me.

Is Exiva an official Tibia app?

No. Exiva is an independent, unofficial fan-made application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by CipSoft GmbH.

Do I need to share my Tibia login?

Never. Exiva does not ask for or store your Tibia credentials. It works with publicly available character and world data.

What works without paying?

You can track 3 characters on your device, search characters, guilds, worlds and houses, browse highscores and daily events, use calculators and search the codex. There are no ads.

Why is 24/7 push a Premium feature?

Free tracking runs on your device and costs us almost nothing. Watching continuously while the app is closed needs servers, polling and addressed push delivery — recurring infrastructure that the subscription funds.

Can I use Exiva offline?

Yes. Cached data remains readable, and Exiva shows exactly how old it is. Premium adds a full offline codex pack.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed through Google Play and can be cancelled there at any time. The final price is always the localised amount shown by Google Play.
