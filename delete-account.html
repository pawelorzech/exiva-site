Account and data deletion

This page explains how to delete your Exiva account and the data linked to it — including when the app is no longer installed. Required by Google Play (Data deletion).

Which app this concerns

Exiva — Android application, package identifier me.orzech.exiva. Developer: Paweł Orzech, pawel@orzech.me.

Free has no server account. A server account is created automatically only when a Premium purchase is verified. It is linked to the Play purchase and an installation identifier, not to an email address or Google profile.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open Exiva on an installation where Premium has been restored.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy and your data → Delete server account.
  3. Read the consequences, tick the acknowledgement and confirm. The active server record is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — by email (no app needed)

Reinstall Exiva from the same Google Play account, use Restore purchase, then follow Option 1. This proves control of the purchase without asking for a password or identity document.

If that is not possible, contact support and include the Google Play order ID shown in your purchase history. Never send a password, full payment-card number or purchase token.

Contact support

Declared time to first response: 7 business days. GDPR requests are fulfilled within 30 days of receipt, extendable by a further 2 months for particularly complex requests — we will tell you about such an extension and its reasons within the first month.

What we delete

Deletion removes the server account, active installation records, entitlement, encrypted purchase token, encrypted notification tokens, server-side tracked-character subscriptions and pending notifications. Exiva currently stores no account email, Google profile identifier, notes, goals or hunt sessions on the server.

What remains despite deletion, and why

DataFor how longWhy
Purchase-token hash, product and base-plan identifiers, detached from the accountUp to 6 yearsminimum accounting and abuse-prevention record; it cannot be decrypted into the purchase token and is not linked to the deleted account
Short-lived infrastructure logs and backupsAccording to the hosting rotation and backup scheduleservice security and disaster recovery; restored data is subject to the same deletion controls

Data on your device

Data stored only on your device is deleted by you — by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings. We have no access to it and cannot delete it for you.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. To end one, go to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Exiva → Cancel subscription.
