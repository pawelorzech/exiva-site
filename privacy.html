Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · 14 August 2026 · applies to Exiva for Android, package me.orzech.exiva

Short version. Free works without an Exiva server account. Most user-created data stays on the device. Premium alerts need a small server record for purchase verification, watched characters and push delivery. Exiva never asks for Tibia or Google passwords and has no advertising.

1. Controller and contact

The controller is Paweł Orzech, trading as YesWas, Poland. Privacy and security contact: pawel@orzech.me. Exiva is an unofficial fan application and is not affiliated with CipSoft GmbH.

2. Data kept only on your device

Tracked-character snapshots, notes and tags, hunt sessions, goals, alert rules, settings, caches and the local export file remain on the device unless a feature explicitly says otherwise. They are stored in Android app-private Room/DataStore storage. You can export the supported local records to JSON, delete them in Settings, clear app data, or uninstall.

3. Data used by public game-data providers

Names and filters you search for are sent to the documented public APIs needed to answer the request, including TibiaData and MediaWiki endpoints listed on Exiva's Data Sources screen. Those providers receive normal network metadata such as an IP address. Exiva does not use private or reverse-engineered game endpoints and never sends Tibia credentials.

4. Premium server account

Verifying Premium creates a pseudonymous server account automatically. It is not an email or social-login account. The server may process:

5. Analytics and crash diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled until the user makes the relevant consent choice in the app. If enabled, Exiva sends technical usage events and diagnostics needed to understand reliability. Event parameters must not contain character names, notes, search text, purchase tokens or notification tokens. Consent can be withdrawn in Settings.

6. Recipients and international processing

Data is processed only by providers required to run the selected function: Google Play for billing, Firebase for app attestation and push delivery, Exiva's VPS/hosting provider for Premium services, and the public game-data providers requested by the user. Exiva has no ads, does not sell personal data and does not send data to data brokers.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local data remains until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall.
  • An active Premium account remains while needed to provide Premium, unless you delete it.
  • Turning off server push invalidates delivery registrations; expiry or revocation removes server tracking and pending delivery work.
  • Deleting the server account immediately removes the account, installations, entitlement, decryptable tokens, watched-character subscriptions and pending notifications.
  • After deletion, only a detached purchase-token hash with product/base-plan identifiers may remain for up to six years for accounting and abuse prevention. It contains no decryptable token or account link.
  • Short-lived operational logs and disaster-recovery backups follow the hosting rotation. They are not used to resume a deleted account.

8. Your controls and rights

Settings → Privacy and your data provides local export, import, analytics consent, local deletion and—when a Premium account exists—direct server-account deletion. The server action and local wipe are deliberately separate, and neither cancels a Google Play subscription.

Instructions for deletion without relying on the current installation are on the account deletion page. You may also contact us to request access, correction, restriction, portability, objection or erasure where applicable. You may complain to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Poland or the authority in your habitual residence.

9. Security

Exiva uses TLS, Android app-private storage, server-side App Check, bounded verification rate limits, HMAC sessions checked against active accounts, encrypted purchase/notification tokens, hashed installation identifiers and least-privilege deployment secrets. No security measure is absolute; report issues through the reporting page.

10. Children

Exiva is not directed to children and does not participate in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. A paid subscription must be purchased through the age and parental controls of Google Play.

11. Changes

Material changes will be described in the app or release notes before they take effect where required. The current version is always published at this URL. Technical implementation, the Play Data Safety declaration and this policy must be kept consistent.
