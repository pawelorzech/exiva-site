Privacy Policy

1. Summary

Exiva is an unofficial fan-made app for Tibia players. It is designed to work without collecting data about you.

  • If you use the free tier, we do not create an account for you and we hold no record about you in our server database. Tracked characters, notes and settings stay on your phone.
  • We never ask for your Tibia login credentials and we never will. All character information comes from publicly available sources.
  • We show no ads and we pass your data to nobody for marketing purposes.
  • We do not sell data. Ever.
  • We do not collect your phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers such as IMEI.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. You can buy and use the subscription without signing in.

4. What we process — Free tier

In the free tier we create no account and no record about you in our server database.

Everything you enter and everything the app collects is stored on your device only, in the app's local database and local preferences:

DataPurpose
Tracked character name and worldshowing the profile, level history and online status
Level and stat history of a tracked charactercharts, summaries, local notifications
Your notes, goals and saved hunt sessionsplanning and analysis features
App settings (theme, language, quiet hours, notification preferences)making the app behave the way you chose
Cached public data (worlds, creatures, items, highscores)speed and offline use

We have no access to any of it. It is not sent to us, it cannot be recovered from us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

The only thing connecting the free tier to our server is a Firebase Cloud Messaging topic subscription, which we use to send news, in-game event information and a daily digest. This is a broadcast: we publish one message to a topic and Firebase delivers it to all subscribers. We cannot see who subscribes to a topic, how many devices there are, or whether a message was delivered. We neither receive nor store your device identifier in connection with these notifications. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.

The free tier also involves the analytics and diagnostics data described in section 7 — collected by Google (Firebase) and not allowing us to identify you.

You can buy and use the subscription without creating an account with us and without signing in. Your entitlement to paid features is then tied to your Google Play account, not to an account with us.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we create an account for you. We do this for two purposes and only those two: (1) so you can restore your subscription after changing devices, (2) so we can synchronise your data across devices.

In addition to the data in 5.1 we then process:

The copy of your data on our server is encrypted, and character names are stored in working tables only as irreversible hashes.

5.3. Declining an account does not remove your subscription

You can sign out at any time and keep using Premium without an account. The only thing you lose is cross-device sync. You can also delete the account — see section 11.

8. Data from public sources

Exiva retrieves information about characters, guilds, worlds, houses and highscores from publicly available sources — primarily the TibiaData API (api.tibiadata.com), which processes data published on the official tibia.com website. Encyclopaedic data comes from TibiaWiki and is made available under the CC BY-SA 3.0 licence. Full list of sources: ATTRIBUTIONS.md.

An important distinction:

We do not collect this data about you — we fetch it because you asked us to. When you enter a character name, the app queries a public source for it. We build no profile of any person from this, we do not link characters to player identities, and we share such combinations with nobody.

A character name is public information, but we treat it carefully. A character name is visible on tibia.com and is in itself a pseudonym. However, combined with your device or account it may indirectly indicate who you are in the game and which characters are yours. Therefore:

  • in our database character names appear primarily as irreversible hashes;
  • full names are stored only inside the encrypted copy of your data;
  • character names are never written to logs in plain text;
  • we do not and will not build a feature showing who tracks which characters.

We never ask for Tibia login credentials. Exiva has no game login screen, accepts no passwords, does not integrate with the game client and does not automate gameplay. If you ever see a screen claiming to be Exiva and asking for your Tibia password — that is not us. Report it to pawel@orzech.me.

13. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including: transport encryption (TLS), encrypted token storage on the device, encryption of the server-side data copy and of backups, storing character names as irreversible hashes, restricting database access to the administrator only and only over an encrypted tunnel, rate limiting, app attestation (Firebase App Check), and automated scanning of code and dependencies for vulnerabilities and leaked secrets.

The full technical description — threat model, data register, retention and incident response procedure — is in docs/SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.md in the project repository.

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the President of the Polish DPA within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and where the risk is high we will also notify you without undue delay.

Security reports are accepted at pawel@orzech.me with a subject line starting with [SECURITY].

14. Children

Exiva is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from persons under 16 years of age. The app is rated in Google Play for adult and teen audiences and does not participate in the Google Play Families / Designed for Families programme.

Tibia is also played by minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal data (which in practice can only happen by creating an account or purchasing a subscription), write to pawel@orzech.me — we will delete it promptly.

Purchasing a subscription requires a Google Play account; control over purchases made by minors rests with Google Play parental settings.

15. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy — for example when we add a feature that requires processing new data, or when the law changes.

  • The current version is always available in the app (Settings → Privacy) and on the project website.
  • Every version carries a number and an effective date.
  • For material changes — those that expand the scope of collected data, change the purpose of processing, or add a new recipient — we will give 14 days' notice: an in-app message, and for account holders also an email.
  • Editorial changes (wording fixes, clarifications, link updates) take effect on publication.
  • An archive of previous versions is available in the project repository history.

Continuing to use the app after changes take effect means you have been informed of them. If you do not accept the changes, you may delete your account and stop using the app; termination of a paid subscription is governed by the Terms of Service.
